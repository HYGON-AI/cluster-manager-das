# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a professional, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of identity,
background, experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and focus discussion on technical facts.
- Welcome questions, constructive review, and differing viewpoints.
- Give and accept feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and the security of users and operators.
- Avoid discriminatory, intimidating, harassing, or disruptive conduct.
- Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, insults, sexualized
language or imagery, sustained disruption, deliberate exposure of confidential
information, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good
faith.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review discussions,
community events, and other spaces where an individual is representing the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's designated private contact channel. Do not include sensitive
personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good
faith, protect confidentiality where reasonably possible, and may edit, reject,
or remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying these expectations fairly and
consistently. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as
a violation.
